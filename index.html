How to Play

Learn in 10 seconds. Every roll matters.

1

Start 8 vs 8

16 tokens sit on the emerald felt table—8 for Player 1 and 8 for Player 2. A fair Coin Toss decides who rolls first.

2

Roll to Steal

Tap the dice box within 15 seconds. Whatever number you roll, you steal that exact number of tokens from your opponent's stack.

3

Watch for Overshoots

Roll higher than your rival's remaining tokens? That's an overshoot—no tokens transfer and the turn passes immediately.

4

Hit 16/16 for Clean Sweep

Land the exact finishing roll to capture all 16 tokens, clinch the Clean Sweep, and share your Victory Card.
